# Hackathon Itinerary

## Pre-Event Phase

### Application Period
**January 8**
- Call for participants circulated across consortium channels
- Application form collects:
  - Team composition
  - Technical approach
  - Intended control signals

---

### Selection & Preparation
**January 9–10**
- Applications reviewed by UK BCI Consortium committee
- Top teams selected
- Notifications sent with preparation materials

**Now - January 15**
- Teams receive:
  - g.tec Unicorn documentation
  - Judging criteria
  - Technical constraints
- Remote development begins

---

## Main Event

### Day 1 — Friday, January 16 (UCL)

**Morning (09:00–12:00)**
- Registration & welcome
- Opening remarks
- Hardware workshop (Unicorn setup)
- Judging criteria briefing
- Q&A

**Afternoon (13:00–17:30)**
- On-site development
- Data collection & testing
- Informal progress check-ins

---

### Weekend — January 17–18
- Remote refinement
- Virtual support channel active
- Presentation preparation

---

### Day 2 — Monday, January 19 (Essex)

**Morning**
- Final testing
- Technical rehearsals

**Afternoon**
- Live performances (20 min/team)
- Judges’ deliberation
- Keynote: *Fostering Creativity via BCI Development*
- Awards & closing remarks

**Evening**
- Networking reception
- Future collaboration discussions

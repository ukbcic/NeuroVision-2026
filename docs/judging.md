# Judging Criteria

Teams are evaluated across five dimensions:

1. **Technical Feasibility**
2. **System Robustness**
3. **Musical Expressivity**
4. **Innovation**
5. **User Experience**

Full scoring rubrics are available to reviewers and committee members only.

---

## Performance Format
- 5 min setup
- 10 min live demonstration
- 5 min Q&A

Backup plans are strongly encouraged.

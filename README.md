# 🎶 UK BCI Consortium Music-BCI Hackathon 2026  
## *Neurovision Music Contest*

> **Where Brain–Computer Interfaces meet musical creativity**

The **UK BCI Consortium Music-BCI Hackathon** brings together **researchers, engineers, musicians, and creatives** to explore **real-time brain–computer interfaces for musical expression**.

Selected teams will design and demonstrate **EEG-controlled musical systems** using the **g.tec Unicorn Hybrid Black**, across a **two-site hybrid hackathon** hosted by **University College London (UCL)** and the **University of Essex**.

## 🧠 What We’re Looking For

We prioritise teams that demonstrate:

- ✅ **Real-time BCI experience**
- ✅ **Functional or near-functional codebases**
- ✅ **Musical creativity and expressive mappings**
- ✅ **Feasible, robust systems under time constraints**

> ⚠️ This is **not** an introductory workshop.  
> Teams should arrive **ready to build, test, and demonstrate**.

---

## 🛠️ Hackathon Format

- **Pre-event remote preparation**
- **On-site development day at UCL**
- **Independent weekend refinement**
- **Live performances & judging at Essex**

Each team is allocated a **g.tec Unicorn system**.
